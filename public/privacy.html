The Love Media

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Last updated 3 November 2026

The Love Media is a small, independent community for LGBTQ+ people to meet, chat and share live video. This page explains what we keep, what stays on your own device, and what you can do about it. It covers the website at the-love-media.vercel.app, the Windows app, and the installed phone app.

The short version

  • We keep your account (codename, hashed password, optional profile) and the videos posted in Welcome and Updates. That is all we store on our servers.
  • Your private messages, friend list, comments and room settings live in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • Live video, voice and screen sharing go directly between the people in the session. We do not record or keep them.
  • No adverts, no analytics, no trackers, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

The Love Media is run by an independent volunteer, not a company. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us through the project's support page on GitHub.

2. What we store on our servers

  • Your account. The codename you choose, your password stored only as a salted hash (a one-way scramble — nobody, including us, can read it back), your password reset key stored the same way, and the profile details you fill in: bio, statuses and connection status.
  • Videos in the Welcome and Updates sections. The clip itself, its title, an optional topic, the codename of the person who posted it, its file size and when it was posted. Only the Gayjesus account can add these videos; everyone can watch them.

That is the complete list. There is no other database of what you do on the site.

3. What stays on your device

Your private messages and saved conversations, your friend list, comments on videos, the community vibe score and any room backgrounds you set are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to us, and other people cannot read them from our servers.

The trade-off is that they belong to that one browser: if you clear your site data, use a different browser, or install the app on a different device, those messages will not be there. We cannot recover them for you. Clearing site data removes them permanently.

4. Camera, microphone, screen sharing and recordings

Your camera and microphone are only switched on when you press the button to start them, and your browser asks for permission first. Live video, voice and screen sharing travel directly between the people taking part, encrypted in transit, and we do not record or store them.

Our server only passes along the small messages needed to connect people and to show who is in a room. That presence information (codename, room name, and how many people are in each room) is held in memory and disappears when you leave.

If you use the record button, the recording is saved to your own device. It is not uploaded anywhere unless you choose to share it elsewhere.

5. Donations and payments

Donations are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us; we do not see or store card numbers. Stripe's privacy policy applies to that part of the service. We only learn that a donation was completed.

6. Technical information

  • IP addresses. When you try to sign in, your IP address is used briefly in the server's memory to slow down repeated failed attempts (a maximum of eight in five minutes). It is not written to our database.
  • Server logs. The companies that host the site keep standard access logs, as every web host does, under their own policies.
  • Browser storage. The app saves settings such as your server address and remembered login in your browser. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies.

7. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no sponsored content.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts of any kind.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with data brokers.
  • We do not use your messages or videos to train AI models.

8. Who processes data for us

The site is kept online by a few free hosting services. They process data only to run the service, under their own privacy terms:

9. How long we keep things

  • Your account is kept until you delete it.
  • Videos are kept until an admin removes them.
  • Sign-in sessions are kept in memory only and expire after eight hours; restarting the server clears them all.
  • Messages and comments on your device stay until you clear your browser data.

10. Your choices and your rights

  • Delete your account. The app has a delete-account option. It removes your codename, password hash, reset key and profile from our database. Videos you posted in Welcome or Updates stay up until an admin removes them — ask us if you want them gone.
  • Clear what is on your device. Clearing your browser's site data deletes your local messages, friends and comments immediately and permanently.
  • Ask us something. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, through the support page. Because an account is only a codename, a hashed password and a short profile, that is a one-paragraph answer.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the UK, or the CCPA in California), you have legal rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to how your data is used. Contact us and we will help.

11. Security

The site uses HTTPS everywhere. Passwords and reset keys are stored only as salted hashes, sign-in sessions are held in memory and expire after eight hours, and repeated failed sign-ins are rate limited. No service is perfectly secure, and a small volunteer-run project cannot promise perfection — but we do not store more than we need, so there is little to lose if something goes wrong.

12. Age

The Love Media is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly keep information about anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change we will also mention it in the app.

14. Contact
